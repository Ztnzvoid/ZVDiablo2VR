# The player's guide

Everything a player needs to install ZVDiablo2VR, play Diablo II: Resurrected in VR with it, and fix it
when something goes wrong.

**The idea: as few menus as possible.** Your body is the interface. Your weapons hang on you, your
inventory is a bag you hold, your potions are on your belt, your life and map are on your wrist, and your
skills ride your gestures. The game's own menus are still there for what has no gesture yet.

Start with **Installing**, play your first sessions on a **new offline character**, and keep **Controls
and gestures** at hand for the first hour.

Each setting is also explained in its own comment in `<game folder>\d2rloader\plugins\horadrim.ini` (this
mod) and `d2r_vr.ini` (natpoh's D2R VR engine) beside it.

ZVDiablo2VR is built on natpoh's [D2R VR](https://github.com/natpoh/diablo2-vr-port) and bearstonem's
[d2r-pcvr](https://github.com/bearstonem/d2r-pcvr). See the [README](../../README.md#thank-you-natpoh-and-bearstonem)
and [CREDITS.md](../../CREDITS.md).
