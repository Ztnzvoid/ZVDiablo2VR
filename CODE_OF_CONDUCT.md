# Code of conduct

ZVDiablo2VR is made by a few people in their free time, for players. Be the kind of person you would
want to meet in a co-op game:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, with examples, never with people. No harassment, insults,
  discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere the project is
  discussed.
- **Be patient and constructive.** Newcomers, players who are not developers and people writing in
  their second language are welcome. Explain; assume good faith.
- **Respect privacy.** Never post anyone's personal data, saves, accounts or logs that are not yours.
- **Respect the authors we build on.** Credit natpoh, bearstonem and everyone in
  [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md); keep their notices.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block anyone who does
not follow this page. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, open an issue asking for a private
contact, without the details.
